Degrees follow standard major-key harmony (1 and 4 and 5 major, 2 and 3 and 6 minor, 7 diminished). "New key each loop" reshuffles the key every time the progression repeats, so you can practice transposing on the fly the way a leader calling an audible would. The metronome accents the first beat of every chord so you can hear the measure, and the keyboard diagram updates live with each chord change.
